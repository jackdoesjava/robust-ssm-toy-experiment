Tolerances are relative to the posterior sd. In float64 the iteration keeps converging until it matches the sequential filter exactly; 1e-8 is a strict target, about the square root of float64 precision. In float32, usual on a GPU, the error stalls at about 1e-6 sd, so only the 1% counts carry over to a GPU.
Delta_t follows an exogenous input here; in the proposal it is computed from the observations. Neither depends on the weights, so the pass structure is the same.
